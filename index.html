Larkspur Instruments

Larkspur Instruments makes bench-top flow meters for small water labs. Each meter reads flow from 0.1 to 40 litres a minute and logs a reading every second to its own storage.

A lab connects a meter over USB or Ethernet. The meter keeps ninety days of readings on the device, and a lab can export them as CSV at any time.
