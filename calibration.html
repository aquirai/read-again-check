Calibration

Every Larkspur meter leaves the factory calibrated against a reference flow rig, and the certificate ships in the box.

We recommend recalibrating once a year. A lab can return the meter to us, or run the field calibration kit, which takes about forty minutes and needs a 10-litre reference vessel.

A meter that drifts more than 2 percent between calibrations is replaced at no charge in its first three years.
